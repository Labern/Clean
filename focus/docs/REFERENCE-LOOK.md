# Focus — the reference look (first version)

Noted 2026-11-14 from his screenshot of the menu drawn on paradoxica.co.uk/focus. His instruction: "I don't want to
stray away too far from this." Nothing here is a change request. Check new menu work against this list.

## The menu, top to bottom
1. **"Focus"**: small grey heading, top left. Nothing on the right.
2. **"Keep open this session — click to choose"**: small grey heading.
3. **App rows**, one per open app: app icon · name · memory (grey, right-aligned, "2.6 GB" / "178 MB") · circle at the far right.
   - Kept apps: white name, white filled circle with a tick.
   - Apps that will close: dimmed name and icon, empty grey circle.
   - Order in the picture: Claude, Terminal (ticked), then Google Chrome, UnrealEditor, Obsidian, System Settings, Keynote, Preview.
4. Thin separator line.
5. **"Critical System Processes — never quit"**: small grey heading.
6. One row per protected app (Wispr Flow): icon · name · white circle with a **lock** at the far right.
7. **"Add or remove…"** with a plus-in-circle icon.
8. Separator.
9. **"Modes — tick the apps you keep open, then save them"**: small grey heading.
10. **"New Mode from Ticked Apps…"** with a plus-in-circle icon.
11. Separator.
12. **Past sessions** (clock-with-arrow icon, chevron), **Tools** (wrench icon, chevron), **Settings** (gear icon, chevron).
13. Separator.
14. **The button**: a white rounded rectangle; "Start New Session" in bold black at the left, "⌘⇧F" in grey at the right.
15. **The caption**, centred grey, three lines: "Starting a new session will save and quit 6 apps, freeing about 12.8 GB of RAM. Click again to reopen them."

## The look
- Off-black menu (#1F1F21) with rounded corners and a faint edge, on a black page.
- Monochrome: white, greys, and one red (Force Quit). No other colour except the apps' own icons.
- Headings: small, semibold, grey, sentence case, with a description after a dash.
- Icons on the left of every command; chevrons on the right for submenus.
- One column of circles at the far right: tick, empty, or lock.
- Generous row height; thin separators between sections.
- The page around it: "★★★★★ × PARADOX" wordmark top left (serif), "Buy" (white button) and "Overview" top right.

## Where today's menu (0.18.10) differs
- Top row: big bold "Focus" with "Quit" at the right.
- "Apps in Session" and "Usage" replace the "Keep open this session…" heading.
- Headings have no descriptions (they show on hover).
- Modes: one-click rows with ⌘ numbers, Create New Mode, Edit Modes, Suggested Modes.
- Tools has headings ("Analyse performance", "Quit Unresponsive Apps") and Analyse comes first.
- The button is taller; "★★★★★ × PARADOX™" sits small at the bottom right.
