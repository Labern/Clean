    🍽 Favourite food

    What they love, what they snub. Tap the hearts to re-rate.
    🚫 Never feed these (genuinely dangerous to cats)
    • Lilies — every part, even pollen or vase water. Kidney failure. Keep them out of the house entirely.
    • Onion, garlic, chives, leeks — damage red blood cells, including powders in gravy and baby food.
    • Chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, xylitol, grapes & raisins.
    • Cow's milk — most cats are lactose-intolerant after weaning. Water is the drink.
    • Raw dough, cooked bones, raw fish in bulk.
    • Paracetamol & dog flea treatments (permethrin) — both can kill a cat.
    • Citrus essential oils — sorry Sol 🍋. Lemons themselves are just unpleasant to cats; the concentrated oils are toxic.

        📹 Kitten cams

        Your Ring cameras, live on this page. Ring has no public streaming API, so this plugs into a small bridge you run at home — the guide below takes ~20 minutes.
        🔧 How to get your Ring cameras on here
        1. Run a bridge at home (any always-on box: Raspberry Pi, NAS, old Mac). Easiest path: ring-mqtt (Docker / Home Assistant add-on). It logs into your Ring account and runs go2rtc inside it, turning every Ring camera into a normal stream. Alternatives: Scrypted (Ring plugin, slick UI) or Home Assistant + its Ring integration.
        2. Expose it over HTTPS. This page is HTTPS, so browsers block plain http:// feeds. Put the bridge behind Tailscale Funnel, a Cloudflare Tunnel, or HA's remote URL.
        3. Paste a URL above. With go2rtc (stream name = camera name):
          • HLS: https://YOUR-HOST/api/stream.m3u8?src=CAMERA — best on iPhone
          • MJPEG: https://YOUR-HOST/api/stream.mjpeg?src=CAMERA — works everywhere
          • Snapshot: https://YOUR-HOST/api/frame.jpeg?src=CAMERA — gentlest on battery cams
          • Embed (WebRTC, lowest lag): https://YOUR-HOST/stream.html?src=CAMERA
        4. Battery Ring cams wake up for every live view and drain fast — use Snapshot at 30s+ for those, live streams for wired ones.
        5. Camera URLs sync to the other phone end-to-end encrypted, like everything else here. Put a password on the bridge anyway.

        🩺 Health check-in

        10 seconds a day. Kittens go downhill fast, so one 😟 brings up the "call the vet" list.
        🚨 Call the vet straight away if…
        • Not eating for more than about 12 hours (tiny kittens can't go long without food)
        • Floppy, unusually sleepy, or hiding and won't come out
        • Vomiting more than once, or diarrhoea for more than a day, or any blood
        • Straining in the tray with little or nothing coming out (urgent)
        • Breathing fast, open-mouth breathing, or pale / white gums
        • Swollen or hard belly, crying in pain, a fall or any injury
        • Thick eye or nose discharge, or sneezing that won't stop

        Out of hours: call your vet's number — it usually redirects to the emergency service.

            Leave one phone or tablet on this page: it watches your Snapshot / MJPEG cams and saves a photo + moment when something moves (at most one every 10 min per camera).
            🔧 Making it work
            • Uses the cameras from 📹 Kitten cams above — add one as Snapshot or MJPEG first.
            • The bridge has to allow the page to read its images (CORS). In go2rtc's config add api: { origin: "*" }. Scrypted / HA: enable CORS for this site.
            • Watching is per-device and only runs while this page is open — an old phone on a charger works well.
