A proper mail app for iCloud.

  1. Use an app-specific password, not your Apple Account password.
  2. Make one at account.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords.
  3. It stays on this device and goes only to your bridge and Apple.

Your own bridge (a free Cloudflare Worker: npx wrangler deploy in post/). Browsers can't speak IMAP directly; the bridge only ever connects to Apple's mail servers and stores nothing.
