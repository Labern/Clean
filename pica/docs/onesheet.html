The screen is the page. Drop in any screenplay PDF and it comes back identical — every line, indent and page break — fully editable, with Final Draft's own typing grammar under your hands. True Courier at true margins: screen, print and PDF are one layout.

It already knows

  • SmartType — characters, locations and times complete from the script itself; in a two-hander it offers the other speaker first. → accepts.
  • int. becomes INT. as you type · ALL-CAPS ending TO: becomes a transition · “(” in dialogue becomes a parenthetical.
  • Auto-(CONT'D) when the same speaker resumes after action.
  • Right-click anything — it names what it is and offers to change it, best guess first. Select a run and press ⇥ or ⏎ to retype it all.
  • Click anywhere — the caret lands at the nearest text, no precision required.

Features

  • Imports the real world — production drafts with revision headers and scene-number gutters, bare or dotted page numbers, even fonts that hide their character map: cracked, scrubbed, reproduced. Proven line-for-line on Tenet: 5,907 of 5,907.
  • Final Draft, both ways — .fdx in and out, lossless, emphasis included.
  • Draft mode — named drafts stored inside the document; compare any draft to the current script scene by scene; restore keeps a safety copy and is undoable.
  • The index — drag to reorder, pin, rename, duplicate, export, delete; each script reopens where you left it.
  • Never lose a word — autosave as you type, rolling snapshots, additive storage that every update must honour.
  • Exports — PDF · Final Draft · Fountain · JSON.
