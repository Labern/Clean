Part 1

Before you start: the PS5

GT7 broadcasts its telemetry on your home network all the time. There's no setting to switch on in the game. You only need three things right:

  1. Same Wi-Fi. The PS5 and your iPhone (or Mac) must be on the same home network. A guest network or a "client isolation" setting on the router blocks it. If your phone has a VPN on, turn it off while playing.
  2. Find the PS5's address. On the PS5: Settings → Network → Connection Status → View Connection Status. Note the IPv4 address, e.g. 192.168.1.23. The app can also find it by itself ("auto"), but typing it is fastest and most reliable.
  3. Be on track. GT7 sends data while you drive and during replays (including the Circuit Experience demo). Menus and loading screens send nothing.
Tip: give the PS5 a fixed address in your router (DHCP reservation) so you never have to look it up again.
Part 2

The iPhone app, with Sideloadly

Apple only lets App Store apps install by themselves, so the app is signed on your Mac with your own Apple ID. Sideloadly does that in one click.

You need

Your Mac, your iPhone, its USB cable, and your Apple ID (a free one works).

  1. Get Sideloadly. On the Mac, download it from sideloadly.io and drag it into Applications. Open it once (if macOS warns, right-click → Open).
  2. Get the app file. Download GT7Endgame.ipa. It's rebuilt automatically every time the app changes.
  3. Connect the iPhone with the cable. Unlock it and tap Trust when it asks about this computer.
  4. Install. In Sideloadly: drag GT7Endgame.ipa into the window, check your iPhone is selected, type your Apple ID email, press Start. Enter your Apple ID password and the 6-digit code if asked. Wait for "Done".
  5. Developer Mode (iOS 16 and later). On the iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → On. The phone restarts; confirm Turn On afterwards. If the option isn't there yet, open the app once and iOS adds it.
  6. Trust yourself as the developer. Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.
  7. Open GT7 Endgame. When it asks to find devices on your local network, tap Allow. This is how it reaches the PS5.
Free Apple ID: the app stops opening after 7 days. That's Apple's rule for free accounts, not the app. Plug in and press Start in Sideloadly again (your notes and laps stay on the phone). Or turn on Sideloadly's automatic refresh so it renews over Wi-Fi when the Mac is on. A paid Apple Developer account ($99 / £79 a year) makes it last a year.
Part 3

Connect to the PS5

  1. Turn on the PS5 and start GT7.
  2. In the app, open Logger (or the Sectors tab, which connects by itself once you've done this once).
  3. Type the PS5 address from Part 1, or leave it empty for auto. Tap Connect to PS5.
  4. Drive. The live panel shows speed, gear, revs, throttle and brake. Every finished lap is saved with GT7's official time; every run between resets (a sector attempt, a demo replay) is saved for the Sector Trainer.

Your GT7 account (optional)

More → PlayStation + GT7 → Sign in to Gran Turismo. Sony's own sign-in page opens inside the app. Afterwards your credits come from the game itself and drive the app's next-move suggestions.

Part 4

The Sector Trainer

  1. Pick the section. Sectors tab → the track → Train this on the section you're doing. Type the gold time GT7 shows (silver and bronze if you want them).
  2. Watch the demo in GT7. The app records it. The first run in a section becomes the demo; if you drove first, tap That was the demo on the right run later.
  3. Drive. Each time you hit retry you get your time, the gap to the demo and to gold, and the corner that cost you most, read out loud.
  4. Calibrate once. Type the time GT7 gave you for one attempt in GT7 said. Every time after that matches GT7's clock.
  5. Look at the data. See it on the logger lays your run over the demo: the map coloured where you gain and lose, both brake points, play both cars together, and speed, gap, brake, throttle, steering and gear traces. Corners are ranked by the time they cost.
  6. Corner notes. Tap a numbered corner chip. You see that corner drawn from the demo with your last attempt, the demo's numbers and every attempt there. Write a note and choose Try it: the app compares that corner in the attempts after your note with the ones before, and tells you whether it worked. The data says suggests notes from patterns in your driving.
Part 5

Without the app: the Mac bridge

Works today from Safari on the phone, as long as the Mac stays on and on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. Install Node.js on the Mac: nodejs.org (LTS), or brew install node.
  2. In Terminal:
    git clone https://github.com/Labern/Clean
node Clean/gt7/bridge/gt7-bridge.mjs --ps5 192.168.1.23
    Use your PS5's address, or --ps5 auto. To test with no PS5: --demo sector.
  3. It prints phone: http://192.168.x.x:8787. Open that address on the iPhone in Safari, add /#/ce for the Sector Trainer. Leave Terminal open while you play.

Your data

Laps, runs and notes are stored on the device. Sign in to the cloud save (More → Cloud save, your PSN ID and a passphrase) to have the same save on the iPhone, the Mac and anywhere else. It's encrypted with your passphrase before it leaves the device.

Updates

The website updates by itself. The iPhone app file is rebuilt automatically whenever the app changes: download it again and press Start in Sideloadly to update (it installs over the top, data kept).
