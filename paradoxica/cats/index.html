How does it work?

Apply for a sign, or put one up

We send you the pack with simple instructions on how to put it up, or just put it up yourself.

A place for drivers to own up

If someone hits an animal, the code on the sign lets them say so, anonymously or honestly. Owners find out what happened instead of waiting and wondering.

Donations go to cat protection charities

The donation code takes you straight there. Every penny goes to charities that protect cats.

Cats Protection

Less weight on other charities

Fewer cats hurt on the road means rescues, shelters and vets have more to give everywhere else.

It helps everybody

As a side effect, there'll be fewer animals killed and less roadkill, meaning there's less of a burden on other charities: fewer dead bodies in the road and less damage to vehicles. It helps everybody, including drivers.

The size of a cat.

Each sign is about the size of a cat, and it lights up when headlights hit it. Instead of catching a cat's eyes in the dark and hitting it, drivers see the sign.

We're not trying to punish drivers. We're trying to stop it happening in the first place.

A request, not a rule.

We're not asking to change the speed limit. We're just asking people to pay attention.

It reduces the workload on the government, on the council and on the driver. It's the simplest, cheapest and best option to save lives, while reducing complexity.

A UK-wide initiative.

The plan:

  • Increase donations to charities
  • Reduce the burden on rescue charities
  • Reduce trauma for owners
  • Allow drivers to clear their conscience without consequence
  • Save the lives of cats

It's a simple idea with a profound effect.
