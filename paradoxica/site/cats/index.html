Slow down for cats.

A small, friendly sign for roads where cats live. It asks drivers to take a little more care. That's it.

How it works
How it works

One sign. One street. Then the next.

1

Cats live on our roads

On plenty of streets, cats are out day and night. A road can carry a 30 limit while its bends and blind junctions are only safe at far less. Most drivers simply don't know the cats are there.

2

A request, not a rule

The sign says "Cats nearby. Please drive carefully." No speed number, no order. It's a neighbour asking.

Its pale yellow is chosen to stay clear of official road-sign colours, so nobody mistakes it for one.

3

Your road gets one

Neighbours club together and order a sign with a small fitting kit. It's sold as close to cost as possible.

4

Put up the right way

Every sign goes up with the local council's agreement. The first pilot is in Seaford, East Sussex, where the town council has already welcomed it.

5

Scan the corner

A small code in the corner of each sign leads here. From it, anyone can support cat charities, find out more, or ask for a sign for their own street.

6

So owners always know

If a driver hits an animal, they can report it through the sign, with their details or anonymously. The owner finds out what happened, instead of waiting and wondering.

7

Not for profit

Any surplus goes back into more signs, and a share goes to cat charities. Slow Down for Cats is run under ★★★★★ × PARADOX.
