## Standing rules — every product, every repo

- **Hit stop.** Labern loves hit stop and wants it used wherever it possibly can
  be. It is a direct descendant of Smash Brothers: at the moment of impact
  everything freezes for a few frames, then the motion carries on. Reach for it by
  default in any animation with a moment of contact: a tick landing, a button
  press, a card dropping, a number arriving, a session starting. Pair it with a
  haptic on a phone. Honour Reduce Motion. Copy this rule into the CLAUDE.md of
  every new project and repo.
- **Graphs you can run a finger along.** On every graph (money, releases, notes
  per day, anything), dragging left or right reads off each data point in turn,
  and the phone gives a haptic tick each time the finger crosses onto another
  point. Labern: "I really like that. It's a premium feel." The figure on show
  changes to the point under the finger and goes back when it lifts.
